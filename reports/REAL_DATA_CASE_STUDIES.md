# Real data case studies

Dataset: 1,003 aligned daily adjusted-close observations from 3 January 2022 through 31 December 2025 for ten US-listed companies and SPY. Source provenance is stored beside the cache and identifies Yahoo Finance via `yfinance`. The default detector found 196 cooldown-separated company events at an absolute abnormal-return z-score threshold of 2.5.

## Case 1 GOOGL and AMZN

GOOGL–AMZN ranked first of 45 ML candidates with edge probability 0.1607 and SRCS 0.2352. The detector found 25 GOOGL events and 17 AMZN events. Shared technology, cloud and digital-advertising exposure are plausible confounders. The probability is below the synthetic calibration cutoff of 0.3005, so the pipeline does not declare an edge.

## Case 2 MSFT and META

MSFT–META ranked second with ML edge probability 0.1273 and SRCS 0.1742. The detector found 24 MSFT events and 15 META events. This is a weak candidate and remains below the synthetic cutoff. Its position is evidence about the model's relative ordering, not proof of a direct economic relationship.

## Ambiguous case NVDA and XOM

NVDA–XOM ranked last by ML probability at 0.0010 despite 15 NVDA events and 17 XOM events; its SRCS was 0.0712. It is an unsuccessful statistical recovery case under the configured feature set and five-day window. It cannot establish the absence of a real economic relationship because daily sampling, model transfer and event definitions may miss one.

The corresponding time-series figures and event-level rows are generated by `python -m experiments.run_real_data`. All three cases remain descriptive statistical evidence rather than causal findings.
